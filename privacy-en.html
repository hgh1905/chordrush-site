ChordRush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

ChordRush (the "app") is a game for practising how to read and play piano chords. The app does not collect personal information and does not send any data over the network. This document explains what information the app handles on your device and how.

1. Personal information we collect

None. The app has no sign-up or login and does not collect names, email addresses, location, advertising identifiers, device identifiers or any other information that could identify you. It contains no analytics tools, no advertising SDKs and no communication with external servers.

2. Microphone use

The microphone is used only when you choose "Microphone" as the input method in Settings. Audio is analysed on the device in real time to detect pitches, and:

  • audio is never saved as a recording;
  • audio never leaves the device;
  • only the analysis result (detected note names) is used to judge your answer;
  • with touch or MIDI input the microphone is not used at all.

3. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored only on your device so that the app can work. It is never transmitted anywhere.

ItemContents
Game settingsSelected chord types, level, display mode, input method and similar options
Play resultsScore, number of correct answers, date played
Microphone calibrationHarmonic ratios and tuning offset of your instrument (numeric values), measured only if you run "Microphone learning"
Microphone diagnostic logFor each judgement in microphone mode, the expected chord and the list of detected note names. No audio is included.

The microphone diagnostic log is passed to an app of your choice through the system share sheet only when you tap the "Share" button yourself. The app never sends the log on its own.

4. Deleting information

Uninstalling the app deletes all stored information. Diagnostic logs can also be deleted individually from the microphone diagnostics screen inside the app.

5. Permissions used

PermissionPurpose
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO)Pitch analysis of your playing in microphone input mode
Audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS)Configuring the audio mode for note playback and microphone input
Foreground service (media playback)Required by the audio library to keep note playback stable

6. Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, and therefore collects none from children.

7. Sharing with third parties

Since no personal information is collected, none is shared with or processed by third parties.

8. Changes to this policy

If new features change how the app handles information, this document will be updated and the effective date changed.
