ChordRush Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 (previous version: November 1, 2026)

ChordRush (the "app") is a game for practising how to read and play piano chords. You can use the app without signing in; in that case it collects no personal information and sends no data over the network. If you choose to sign in, your play records are stored on our server and you can join the ranking. The information collected in that case is described below.

1. Users who do not sign in

Nothing is collected. The app does not collect registration details, names, email addresses, location, advertising identifiers, device identifiers or any other information that could identify you, and it contains no analytics tools or advertising SDKs. Opening the ranking screen downloads the public ranking list from the server, but no information about you is sent.

2. Users who sign in (optional)

The following information is collected only if you choose Google, Kakao or Naver sign-in on the "Account" screen.

ItemDetailsPurpose
Social account identifierThe unique user ID issued by the sign-in provider (Google/Kakao/Naver). Only this ID is stored; the app never has access to your provider password.Identifying you and linking records when you sign in again
Display nameThe nickname (name) from your provider profile. You can change it in the app.Shown on the ranking
Play recordsScore, hits and misses, accuracy, max combo, level, chord types, date playedKeeping your history, restoring it on a new device, computing the ranking
Sign-in timesAccount creation date and last sign-in timeService operation

We do not request or store your email address, phone number, date of birth or profile photo. Only your display name and scores are visible on the ranking; the social account identifier is never shown.

3. Microphone use

The microphone is used only when you select "Microphone" as the input method in Settings. Audio is analysed in real time on the device, for pitch detection only:

  • Audio is never saved as a recording.
  • Audio never leaves the device, whether or not you are signed in.
  • Only the analysis result (detected note names) is used for judging the game.
  • With touch or MIDI input the microphone is not used at all.

4. Information stored on your device

The following is stored on your device so the app can work. Except for play results, none of it is sent anywhere.

ItemDetails
Game settingsSelected chord types, level, display mode, input method, language, etc.
Play resultsScore, hits, date played. Also stored on the server if you are signed in.
Sign-in sessionIf signed in, the authentication token issued by the server and your display name
Microphone calibrationOvertone ratios and tuning offset of your instrument (numeric values) measured when you run "Learn my piano"
Microphone diagnostic logFor microphone mode, the expected chord and detected note names of each judgement. No audio is included.

The diagnostic log leaves the device only when you tap "Share" yourself, through the device share sheet, to an app you choose.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Information on the device is removed when you uninstall the app. Records and diagnostic logs can also be deleted inside the app.
  • Account information and play records on the server are kept until you delete your account. Tapping Delete account on the app's "Account" screen immediately deletes the account and all records stored on the server. If you cannot use the app, follow the account deletion instructions to request deletion by email; requests are handled within 7 days.
  • Signing out alone keeps your server records; they are restored when you sign in again with the same social account.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share the collected information with third parties. When you sign in, our server verifies the token issued by the sign-in provider (Google LLC, Kakao, Naver) with that provider; this communication is governed by each provider's privacy policy. The server is operated by the developer and all traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.

7. Permissions used

8. Children's privacy

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. Children under 14 are advised not to use the sign-in feature, and any account found to belong to a child is deleted without delay.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way information is handled changes, this document is updated and the effective date changed. Revision of November 20, 2026: added sections for optional sign-in, server-side records and the ranking feature.
