Delete your ChordRush account (English)

In the app (immediate): open ChordRush, tap Account (your name) on the home screen, tap Delete account, then confirm with Really delete. Your account and all play records on the server are deleted immediately. Records on your device stay and can be cleared from the History screen.

By email: if you cannot use the app, email HansAIsolution@gmail.com with the sign-in provider you used (Google/Kakao/Naver) and the name shown on the ranking. After verification we delete the account within 7 days and reply.

What is deleted: the social account identifier, display name, account timestamps and every play record stored on the server (they also disappear from the ranking). Nothing is retained afterwards. See the privacy policy for details.
